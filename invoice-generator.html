👋 Welcome to Invoice Generator

🏢
Set up your business once
Type your business name and details at the top of the invoice, and add your logo. These are remembered for every future invoice.
✏️
Fill the invoice directly on the page
Everything on the white sheet is editable — client details, dates, line items. Amounts, totals, tax and the amount-in-words update instantly as you type.
🧾
Numbering is automatic
Each new invoice gets the next number (INV-0001, INV-0002…). Use ⧉ Duplicate to reuse a previous invoice for a repeat client.
💰
Tax and discounts
Set the tax percentage (label it GST, VAT — whatever applies) and an optional discount in the totals box. Currency is switchable in the toolbar.
🖨️
Print or save as PDF
Hit 🖨 Print / PDF and choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog — you get a clean, professional document with none of the app buttons.
📋
Track your invoices
Every invoice is saved in this browser and listed on the left with its status — mark them Sent or Paid as you go.
