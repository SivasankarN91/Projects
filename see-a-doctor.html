Without a key the app works fully — you tap through the questions. With one, you can type freely and it finds the right section for you.

What leaves your device

  • Without a key, nothing ever leaves this device.
  • With a key, what you type is sent to Anthropic to be read. Do not type names or anything you would not share.
  • Each check costs a small amount on your own account.
It still does not diagnose. The AI only works out which symptoms you described. How urgent it is stays with the same offline rules, and the AI can only ever make the answer more urgent, never less.
