👋 Build your online shop in minutes

📦
Add your products
Tap ＋ Add product — add several photos, a price, category, and options like Size or Colour. Mark bestsellers, feature them, or set a stock count for “Only 2 left!”.
🏪
Set up your store
In Store settings add your name, logo, brand colour, WhatsApp number (where orders arrive), delivery charges, and discount codes.
👀
Preview the real shop
The Preview tab shows exactly what customers get — search, sort, wishlist, add to cart, apply a code, and test the WhatsApp order right there.
🛒
Customers order on WhatsApp
Shoppers add items (with their chosen size/colour) and hit Order on WhatsApp — the full order, discount and delivery open as a message to you. Add a UPI ID for one-tap payment too.
🚀
Export & go live
Export store downloads your shop as a single file — upload it to any free host (GitHub Pages, Netlify) and share the link. No monthly fees.
