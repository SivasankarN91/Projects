👋 Welcome to Data Analyzer

📂
Load your data
Drop any CSV file on the page, paste rows copied from Excel/Sheets, or hit 🎲 Sample data to look around first. Your data never leaves the browser.
🏷️
Columns are understood automatically
Numbers, dates and text are detected, and each column card up top shows its stats — totals, averages, ranges, most common values, and missing cells.
🔍
Sort, search and filter
Click a column heading to sort. Use the boxes under the headings to filter — text search, number ranges, date ranges, or value pickers. The global search box scans every column.
📊
Summarize and chart
In Analyze, pick what to group by (e.g. Region) and what to show (e.g. Sum of Revenue). You get a summary table and a chart together — and they respect your active filters.
⬇️
Export anything
Download the filtered rows or the summary as CSV, or the chart as a PNG image.
💾
It remembers
Smaller datasets and your filters are saved in this browser, so a reload picks up where you left off. Reopen this guide anytime with ?.
